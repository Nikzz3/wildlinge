# Wildlinge – Roster

All 44 Wildlinge of Alvenland, in Lexikon order. Stats, moves and Lexikon texts live in
`src/data/species.ts`; this file is the creative brief for designers and the pixel artist.

Art notes that apply to every entry:

- Front sprite 48×48 (enemy view), back sprite 64×64, icon 24×24. "Size" says how much of the
  48×48 box the front sprite should fill.
- Light from the top left. Outlines are a darkened version of the neighbouring fill, never pure black.
- The listed hex colors are the main palette; add 1–2 shade/highlight tones per color as needed.
- Each line keeps a recognizable motif across its stages, but every species must read differently
  in silhouette alone.

Habitats follow the story route: Moosbach → Pfad 1 → Kieselstadt → Pfad 2 → Flüsterwald → Seewil →
Pfad 3 → Glimmerhöhle → Funkenstadt → Pfad 4 → Frostalp → Gipfelpfad → Gipfelhorn.
Fully evolved Wildlinge appear in the wild no earlier than Pfad 3 (and two-stage finals with
stat totals above 430 not before Pfad 4), so wild creatures never outclass the starters.

## #001 Sprössli (`sproessli`)

- **Types:** Pflanze
- **Evolution:** → Moosdachs at Lv 16
- **Habitat:** Starter from Professorin Arven's lab in Moosbach (not found in the wild)
- **Look:** A badger cub sitting on its haunches – low, round body, a wide wedge-shaped head and
  stubby legs. Where a badger has its two dark face stripes, two long living grass blades grow
  from the nose over the head and lie flat down the neck, framing a cream stripe. The tips curl
  loose behind the ears. Its short tail is a stiff tuft of grass.
- **Size:** small; about 55 % of the box.
- **Colors:** sage fur `#8a9a6a`, dark fur `#3e5a34`, cream stripe/belly `#f0ecd8`,
  grass blades `#68c048`, nose `#4a3a34`.
- **Pose:** sitting, head tilted, grass-blade tips flicking, one forepaw lifted as if waving.

## #002 Moosdachs (`moosdachs`)

- **Types:** Pflanze
- **Evolution:** Sprössli Lv 16 → Moosdachs → Dornachs at Lv 34
- **Habitat:** Starter line only
- **Look:** A sturdy badger on all fours. The two grass blades have widened into two thick bands
  of moss that run from the nose over the back to the tail, dotted with tiny white star flowers;
  the fur between them stays sage. Broad forelegs end in pale digging claws, often caked with soil.
- **Size:** medium; about 70 % of the box, wider than tall.
- **Colors:** moss `#5f7a46`, dark stripes `#2e4228`, cream face/belly `#d8d8b0`, flowers
  `#f8f4e8`, claws `#8a6a48`.
- **Pose:** four-legged, head low and pushed forward, sniffing the ground.
